This is the official company presentation about АО «НК «Қазақстан темір жолы» (KTZh / Kazakhstan Temir Zholy) — the national railway company of Kazakhstan. It serves as the corporate background for the hackathon case you shared earlier (the locomotive digital twin dashboard).
Slide-by-Slide Summary
Slide 1 – Title

Logo of KTZh.
Headline: Қазақстан темір жолы (Kazakhstan Temir Zholy).
Subtitle: Transport and logistics framework of the country.
Visual: Globe highlighting Kazakhstan’s rail network in yellow.

Slide 2 – More than just a railway

KTZh is a global transport-logistics holding.
Three main activities:
Management of the main railway network
Cargo and passenger transportation
Provision of complex logistics services

Sole shareholder: АО «Самрук-Қазына» (national sovereign wealth fund).

Slide 3 – Scale and strategic role

64% of the country’s total cargo turnover.
Supports > 100 000 jobs.
Connects 4 key macroregions: China, Europe, Central Asia, Persian Gulf countries.
Actively developing major transit corridors: East–West and North–South.

Slide 4 – Kazakhstan: Global transit hub

Large interactive map showing Kazakhstan as the central link between:
Europe ↔ China (via land and sea ports)
Russia, Central Asia, Iran, India, etc.

Highlights key ports (Aktau, Kuryk, Baku, etc.) and rail corridors.

Slide 5 – Anatomy of the holding: How the KTZh mechanism works

Concentric-circle diagram showing the company structure:
Core (1): Infrastructure (main railway network, traffic management, integrated planning).
2. Cargo – KTZh Freight Transportation, Kaztemirtrans, etc.
3. Logistics – Kedentransservice, sea ports (Aktau, Kuryk).
4. Passengers – KTZh Passenger Transportation, passenger locomotives.
5. Security – KTZ-Express, military protection, heat/water supply.


Slide 6 – Assets

16 006 km of main railway network.
1 731 locomotives.
49 859 units of rolling stock (47 641 freight wagons + 2 218 passenger cars).
Additional infrastructure:
10 transshipment points
1 dry port
1 terminal in Lianyungang (China)
2 sea ports + 1 sea terminal
2 ships


Slide 7 – Driving force: People of KTZh

118 507 employees — one of the largest employers in Kazakhstan and a major social driver of the country.

Slide 8 – KTZh on the world stage: Global rankings

Top-20 worldwide in track length → 19th place (16 061 km).
Top-10 worldwide in cargo turnover → 6th place (261 729 million t-km).
Top-10 worldwide in number of locomotives → 7th place (1 913 locomotives).
Note: Operations exceed many developed European and Asian countries.

Slide 9 – Systemic architecture of the future

Closing message:“KTZh is a systemic company that ensures the transportation of goods and passengers in the interests of national security and the economic development of the country.”

Quick Takeaways (for the hackathon context)

KTZh is huge — national strategic asset, major global transit player, heavy focus on digital transformation (which explains why they’re running a hackathon for a real-time locomotive health dashboard).
The digital twin you’re building directly supports their core needs: safety, reliability, predictive maintenance, and operational efficiency of the 1 731 locomotives on 16 000+ km of track.
